Support

Roundtable
Questions, bugs or ideas? Email developtonyt@icloud.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (Settings → About).

Restoring a purchase

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy. Purchases are managed by Apple; refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Correcting or deleting scores

During a game, open Rounds and tap a round number to edit it, or choose Undo last round. To remove a finished game, open Game history, swipe left on the game, and confirm Delete game.

Continuing your game

Saved rounds return when you reopen the app. In a finished result, choose Play again for a fresh score sheet with the same players and rules. Finish or discard an active game before starting another.

Offline play

Scoring and saved history work offline. Buying or restoring Pro requires access to the App Store. Keep your regular device backups; deleting the app can remove its local scores.

Your data

Roundtable does not collect any data about you.
