Privacy Policy

Roundtable · Effective September 26, 2026

Short version: Roundtable does not collect any data about you.

Data stored on your device

Everything you create in Roundtable is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and is included in your normal iCloud or computer device backups, which Apple manages under its own privacy policy. We have no server and never receive this data.

No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking

Roundtable has no sign-in, contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details; the app only asks Apple whether a purchase is active on your Apple Account.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, and use it only to reply.

Children

Roundtable does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
