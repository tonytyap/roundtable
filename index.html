Roundtable

Keep score together. Keep the game moving.

A clear, offline score sheet for your next card or board game night. Add your players, enter each round’s points, and see who’s ahead.

Every round counts

Choose highest score, lowest score, or a target. Record positive, negative, and zero scores. Correct an earlier round or undo the last one before you finish.

One more game?

Keep finished results in history and start a rematch with the same players and rules. Scores save on your iPhone. No account, ads, or internet connection needed to play.

Free to start

Play with up to four players and revisit your five latest finished games. Older games stay saved. Roundtable Pro is an optional one-time purchase for unlimited players and full history. Your local price appears in the app. No subscription.
